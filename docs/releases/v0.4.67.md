# 0.4.67 — Career records, SC and attribution fixes

- Add highest single-match kills, best K/D and highest deaths to the selected history period. Click a record to open that match.
- Achievement details show the last observed acquisition time, or the acquisition match date for older records. Ribbon details show the largest saved count in one match. Double-click an award to open its corresponding match.
- Use **SC (Super Credits)** as the default display unit at **1 SC = 1 USD**, as requested by the author. Existing saved MC selections map to SC; explicit USD/CNY/EUR/GBP/SEK selections remain. Original integer monetary records and real currency rates are preserved. This is a MOD display rule.
- Restore the existing Impaler ribbon for its reviewed full-body resource. Reuse the approved artwork, keep tentacles excluded and retain local kill-credit requirements. The named heavy/giant roster audit found no other missing ribbon mappings.
- Fix bounded ground-fire attribution for targets first observed at their fatal fire state. Preserve confirmed fire areas across the owner's death within their original expiry. Repeated dead-state polls no longer clear pending native kill credits; Health/lifecycle death and delayed native death counters are deduplicated. Add fixed-size diagnostic counts to distinguish missing events, evidence and rejected attribution. Known foreign/direct sources, native kill quotas, radius/expiry limits and history are preserved.

See [Career records](../career-records-0.4.67.md), [SC conversion](../sc-currency-0.4.67.md), [ribbon audit](../unit-ribbon-coverage-0.4.67.md) and [G-8 diagnosis](../g8-ground-fire-0.4.67.md). These fixes have offline replay coverage; actual multiplayer unknown-source rates require a new live match. No historical kills or costs are rewritten.
